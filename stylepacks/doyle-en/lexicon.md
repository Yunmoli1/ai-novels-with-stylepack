# Arthur Conan Doyle — lexicon (en)

> Rates are per 1,000 words over 105,301 words of Doyle short fiction; counts are raw.
> These are the words a generator should reach for by reflex in this pack.

## Function-word floor

The pack is ordinary English at the bottom and Doyle at the layer above it. Do not try to
sound Victorian by inflating the top: the articles and pronouns do the work, in the
proportions below.

## Sentence moulds

Attested shapes, in order of usefulness:

- `It is a capital mistake to ...` — a maxim with a judgement word in it.
- `There is nothing more deceptive than ...` — comparison-maxim, often laughed off.
- `Pray + verb` — `Pray take a seat`, `Pray continue your very interesting statement`.
- `I confess that ...` — the narrator owning an error.
- `I fancy that ...` / `I am sure that ...` — hedged narrator inference.
- `You see, but you do not observe.` — axiom, restatement, instance.
- `Quite so.` / `No doubt.` / `Of course.` — one-word agreements that keep a dialogue moving.
- `It appears that ...` / `It seems that ...` — reported fact, second hand.
- `... , and that is why ...` — the chain completed in one clause.
- `What do you make of it?` / `What do you deduce from it?` — the question that hands the
  floor to the detective.
- `A singular case` / `a remarkable case` / `an extraordinary case` — the case named
  before it is described.

## Names, numbers, money, titles

- Titles: `Mr`, `Mrs`, `Miss`, `Dr`, `Inspector`, `Colonel`, `the Count`, `your Majesty`.
  Abbreviated with a full stop: `Dr. Watson`, `Mr. Holmes` in narration.
- Money in sovereigns, shillings, half-crowns, guineas: `half a crown a packet`, `a
  thousand a year`, `fifty guineas`.
- Measurements spelled out with fractions: `seven and a half pounds`, `six feet six
  inches`, `two miles and a half`.
- Times by the clock only when a timetable matters; otherwise light, meals and cabs.
- Place names are specific and dull: `Brixton Road`, `Lauriston Gardens`, `Saxe-Coburg
  Square`, `Briony Lodge`, `Baker Street`.

## Italics

Only 38 italic spans in 105,301 words. Latin, legal and foreign tags carry them:
`_locus standi_`, `_carte blanche_`, `_incognito_`, and single emphasised words
(`she is always _the_ woman`). One per few thousand words; never for emphasis of tone.

## Spelling

British throughout: `-ise` (55) against `-ize` (13); `grey` 24, `gray` 0; `colour` 19,
`honour`, `neighbour`, `practise` as verb, `defence`, `travelling`. Use `learnt` and
`spelt` where the period would; avoid American `-or`, `-er`, `-og`.

## Banned in this pack

- Straight quotes `"`, ellipsis `...` and `…`.
- American spelling and American idiom (`okay`, `guy`, `awesome`, `realize`, `gray`).
- Said-bookisms and adverb-heavy tags.
- Therapeutic and psychological vocabulary (`trauma`, `closure`, `toxic`, `energy`).
- Modern procedural vocabulary (`forensics`, `DNA`, `profile` as a verb).
- Present-tense narration and free indirect style.
