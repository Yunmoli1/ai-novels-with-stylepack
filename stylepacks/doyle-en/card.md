Arthur Conan Doyle, English. Watson narrates in the first person, retrospective and dry; Holmes reasons aloud in clipped declaratives; dialogue carries the plot. Median sentence thirteen words; one room or one street per scene; fog and gaslight keep the hour. Victorian British diction — upon, quite, pray, singular, no doubt. Em dashes interrupt, never ellipses. Ban modern idiom, American spelling, straight quotes, psychologising.
